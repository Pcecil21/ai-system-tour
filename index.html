Three things it's doing for me
01

A compliance manual that didn't eat my weekends.

Writing an RIA compliance manual from scratch is eighty-plus hours of solo grinding. Most advisors either put it off or pay $15,000 for a boilerplate template and spend weeks trying to make it fit.

I drafted mine section by section with Claude as a research assistant that actually understood my custodian structure and the specific regulations I care about. It got written in evenings. My family barely noticed.

02

Trade rationales that don't sit in drafts.

A compliant trade rationale takes focused writing — the kind you kick down the road when the week fills up. I used to. Rationales would stack up. Compliance would slip.

Now the note gets drafted from the trade details in under a minute. I review, I edit, I sign. Nothing stacks up.

03

Portfolio drift I catch in days, not quarters.

Drift in a client portfolio used to go unseen until the next quarterly review — two or three months of misalignment before anyone noticed.

Every morning, a scan runs across all twelve households, flags anything above threshold, and hands me a ranked list by 7am. Issues get caught in days, not quarters.
