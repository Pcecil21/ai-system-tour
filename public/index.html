01 — Compliance

A compliance manual that didn’t eat my weekends.

Saved ≈ 80 hours

The compliance manual was the thing I kept not starting. Every advisor going independent faces it — 80+ hours of work, or $15,000 for a boilerplate template you spend weeks reshaping to fit your practice.

I wrote mine section by section over six weeks of evenings. Claude researched the regs specific to my custodian structure, drafted each section, and flagged the judgment calls that had to be mine. It got done. My family barely noticed.

02 — Trading

Trade rationales that don’t sit in drafts.

Under 60 seconds

Compliant trade documentation requires focused writing — the kind that slides to Friday when the week fills up. Mine were stacking. Compliance was slipping.

Now the rationale drafts itself from the trade details in under a minute. I review it, edit it, sign it. Nothing stacks. I’m not starting every week already behind.

03 — Monitoring

Portfolio drift I catch in days, not quarters.

12 households · daily

Drift used to be invisible until the quarterly review — two or three months of misalignment before it surfaced. By then the client conversation is reactive instead of proactive.

Every morning a scan runs across all twelve households, flags anything above threshold, and hands me a ranked list by 7am. Issues surface in days, not quarters.

Philosophy — in one breath
I used to spend half my week on work that didn’t require an advisor. So I built a system that does it for me.
