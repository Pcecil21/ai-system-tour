02 — About me

I spent 22 years with real money on the line.

Before I ever managed a portfolio, I traded U.S. Treasury options — starting in the open-outcry pit and moving upstairs as the markets went electronic. For most of those years I worked as a risk manager, responsible for the desk and the traders on it.

That’s not a line on a résumé. It’s the reason I read a portfolio differently than most advisors do. I’ve watched what markets do under real stress — not in a textbook, in real time, with capital on the line. I know the difference between a number on a screen and a position you have to live with.

After two decades, I had to be honest with myself: I was good at the work, but it didn’t help anyone. Trading Treasury options is a closed loop — capital moves from one screen to the next, and no one’s life is better for it. The part I genuinely liked was the part away from the desk: helping real people make real decisions about their money. So I left to do that full-time — and brought the risk discipline with me.

Most advisors have never had a dollar of risk on. I did it for two decades. That judgment is what you’re hiring.

01 — Your trades

Every trade in your account, explained. Same day.

In plain English

When I make a move in your portfolio, you don’t get a vague line item you notice three weeks later. You get a clear, written reason the same day — what changed, why I acted, and what it means for you.

Most advisors mean to document their thinking and run out of week. Mine never stacks up, so the reasoning behind your money is always there when you want to see it.

02 — Your portfolio

Your portfolio, watched every morning. Not every quarter.

Drift caught in days

At most firms, drift hides until the quarterly review — two or three months of slow misalignment before anyone looks. By then the conversation is reactive instead of ahead of it.

Here, every account is scanned before the market opens and anything off-target is flagged that day. Problems surface in days, not quarters — while they’re still small.

03 — You & me

When you call, you reach the trader. Not a queue.

Fee-only fiduciary

No call center. No junior rep reading from a script. No product quota behind the advice. You reach the person who spent two decades managing risk on a trading desk.

And because I’m fee-only — paid by you, not by what I sell you — my only incentive is your outcome. As a fiduciary, that’s not a slogan. It’s the standard I’m held to.

Your data

The honest answer on AI and your private information.

Most advisors dodge this with “privacy rules won’t let us.” Here’s the real answer: in this business, client data usually gets exposed the boring way — an email attachment, a laptop open at the coffee shop. I built the opposite of that.

Anthropic never trains on your data

I work under Anthropic’s commercial terms — your information is never used to train an AI model. That’s the contract in writing, not just my word for it. It does the task in the moment; it doesn’t learn from your money.

Your name comes off before your data does

Identifying details are stripped and reviewed by me before your financial data goes to the model. It works on the numbers, not your identity.

Your records live locked down, not scattered

Encrypted files on my own machines, with access controls — not spread across a dozen consumer apps and inboxes the way it usually is.

A hard block-list in the tools I build

The automations I write check a deny-list of sensitive terms before any outbound search or call goes out — a seatbelt wired into the tooling itself.

For 22 years my job was protecting a desk from the risk nobody else saw coming. I treat your data the same way.
