# Under the Hood — Differentiation Reframe

## Summary

Reframe the Under the Hood page around **speed · accuracy · thoroughness** — one fiduciary delivering what a 10–20 person operation produces — recasting the existing technical inventory as the *proof* of that capacity, plus a compact "most advisors vs. this practice" contrast.

---

## Problem Frame

The page currently lists *what the system is* (110+ playbooks, 34 specialists, ~30 projects) without telling a sophisticated prospect what it does for them or why it beats their current advisor. A high-net-worth reader — the kind otherwise served by a senior advisor at a William Blair or a principal at Rothschild — comes away thinking "impressive tooling," not "this is why I'd move my money here."

At a big firm, that reader's account passes through an analyst, a trader, compliance, ops, a paraplanner, a CSA, and a tax specialist — every handoff a delay, every seam a place something slips. The page makes neither that pain nor its absence here visible.

---

## Requirements

**Framing layer (new, top of page)**
- R1. Add a differentiation section after the hero/intro and before the technical deep-dive, built on the spine **speed · accuracy · thoroughness**.
- R2. State the capacity thesis plainly: one principal — a 22-year trader and fiduciary — delivering what a 10–20 person operation produces; name the functions collapsed into one (analyst, trader, compliance, ops, paraplanner, CSA, tax).
- R3. Include a compact head-to-head contrast (table), reusing the already-established, defensible differentiators re-cut to the speed/accuracy/thoroughness lens:

  | Most advisors | This practice |
  |---|---|
  | A relationship manager who's never traded | 22 years running a trading desk |
  | Reviews your portfolio quarterly | Every account scanned every morning |
  | Documents trades when there's time | Written rationale, same day, every trade |
  | You reach a rotating rep or call center | You reach the principal — I answer |
  | Capped by one person's hours | One fiduciary + a system that never sleeps |
  | Paid by fees and product commissions | Fee-only — no product shelf |

- R4. Graft the capacity beat: frame the 34 specialists / playbooks as "the functions a firm staffs with salaries — collapsed into one," operating under the advisor's direction.

**Proof layer (existing content, reframed)**
- R5. Give each existing technical section a one-line "what this means for you" lead, without rewriting the section's content.
- R6. Recast the intro from "a practice-management system built on Claude" to lead with the capacity/outcome.

**Guardrail**
- R7. Keep the human principal in front throughout — the system amplifies judgment; nothing may imply a bot manages client money.

---

## Success Criteria

- A sophisticated prospect scanning the page can state in one sentence what they get (the speed, accuracy, and thoroughness of a 10–20 person operation from one fiduciary) and why it beats their current advisor.
- The technical inventory reads as *proof* of the differentiation, not a feature brag.
- No claim implies AI replaces the advisor's judgment, and no fabricated comparative/performance figures appear.
- A downstream implementer can wire the reframe from this doc without inventing new positioning or claims.

---

## Scope Boundaries

- The org-chart visual (Approach A) and a full "day in your account" narrative (Approach C) as primary structures — only A's capacity *beat* is grafted.
- Rewriting the playbook / specialist showcase cards.
- The queued dramatic-visual phases (B-band image, inventory hero image, motion overhaul) — separate track.
- Any new comparative or performance claims beyond what is already established and defensible.
- The client landing page — it already carries this positioning and is not reworked here.

---

## Key Decisions

- **Direction B + grafts** over A (org-chart) or C (narrative): B uses the user's own framing (speed/accuracy/thoroughness), is client-outcome-first, and keeps the human in front — the lowest "bot runs my money" risk — while still grafting A's strongest capacity beat and a compact contrast.
- **Copy/structure only; no new imagery required** — keeps this reframe decoupled from the visual track so it can ship independently.

---

## Dependencies / Assumptions

- Reuses the differentiators already established on the client landing page; assumes those remain accurate and defensible for Pete (fiduciary, fee-only, 22-yr trader, daily monitoring, same-day rationales, direct access).
