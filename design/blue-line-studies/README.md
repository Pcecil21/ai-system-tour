# The blue line through the work

Two conceptual editorial renderings for Under the hood, created September 6, 2026 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. They replace the generic book/coffee and paper image bands. They depict an idea about research and organized knowledge; they are not photographs of Pete's workspace or records.

## Working brief

The tour helps prospective advisory clients and technically curious visitors understand the tools behind the practice and reach Contact Pete. Its cream canvas, cobalt accents, DM Sans/Newsreader typography and factual catalog remain. The authentic family and founder photography is the strongest human element and is preserved.

The earlier bands had three visible limitations: generic office/lifestyle subjects, amber color that felt separate from the cobalt identity, and portrait source images heavily cropped into panoramic bands. This pass targets the two bands, preserving all copy, controls, current project examples and homepage composition.

The first scene turns layered cotton-paper leaves into a central arch crossed by one cobalt ribbon. The second uses the same tactile paper and cobalt material in a more ordered archive with dark channels. Raking light and physical texture provide depth. The main gestures remain central for phone crops. No added motion, rasterized interface, fictional financial data or new dependencies.

## Integration

The research band uses a 3:1 desktop aspect ratio; the quieter memory band uses 3.6:1. Phone bands retain 1.8:1. The wider memory revision shows complete dividers and compartments; its 57% vertical crop positioning preserves the base in the shallow desktop band. `sizes` accounts for the source width needed to cover the narrower mobile shape. Intrinsic dimensions, reserved aspect ratios, native lazy loading, async decoding and decorative accessibility treatment remain. The old gradient wash is removed from these two scenes to retain their lighting and blue color.

Current verification and preview are in the latest root HANDOFF entry. All prior image files remain available. The homepage, authentic photos, factual content, scripts and navigation are preserved.
